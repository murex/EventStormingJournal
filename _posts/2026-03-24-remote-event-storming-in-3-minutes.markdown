## Why Go Remote with Event Storming?

Logistics and finance make it hard to repeat Co-Located Event Storming regularly. Remote Event Storming solves this by offering important long-term benefits, such as:

*   Saving time and money
*   Being greener and more inclusive
*   Leveraging digital tools for better outcomes

## Simplify Remote Event Storming With 7 Essential Practices

![Remote Event Storming Facilitation is quite simple once you get used to a few fundamental practices. And it's like riding a bike, you never forget how to do it.]({{site.url}}{{site.baseurl}}/imgs/2026-03-24-remote-event-storming-in-3-minutes/simple-bike.jpg)

Self-organization won’t happen by itself online. As the facilitator, you will need to make the implicit explicit. The 7 key practices are:

*   Splitting the workshop into short 90-minute sessions
*   Breaking out into topic rooms
*   Assigning specific collaboration roles
*   Merging work with Silent Sorting
*   Keeping the board self-explanatory
*   Having participants practice first
*   Finding a co-facilitator

## Your Step-by-Step Guide to Remote Event Storming

Proper preparation limits stress during the workshop. It involves:

*   Choosing an infinite canvas tool
*   Securing an enthusiastic co-facilitator
*   Setting up a template-based board
*   Running a tech-check session

During the workshop, use breakout rooms and Silent Sorting to organize events. Replace the usual storytelling format with subgroup board-section walkthroughs to maintain momentum.

## Leverage the time between sessions in a remote Event Storming

![Taking a pause between sessions allows participants to recharge and facilitators to review and adjust the workshop.]({{site.url}}{{site.baseurl}}/imgs/2026-03-24-remote-event-storming-in-3-minutes/pause.jpg)

Short, 90-minute sessions prevent video fatigue and give you a facilitation superpower! Between sessions, you can:

*   Review feedback and tidy the board.
*   Create snapshots to keep an audit trail.
*   Adjust the next agenda based on new findings.

Participants can do homework and return ready for the next step.

## Remote Event Storming: Making Virtual Workshops Fun and Productive

Maintaining attention online requires active participation:

*   Establish clear rules and mandate webcams.
*   Maximize breakout room usage.
*   Name people directly when asking questions.
*   Embrace regular screen breaks.
*   Keep the energy high with short, relevant energizers.
